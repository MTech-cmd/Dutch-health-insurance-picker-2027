# Noordlicht Zorg — Fictional Wiki

> **Fictional training entry.** This is not a real company or a real Wikipedia article.

## Overview
Noordlicht Zorg is a fictional Dutch health insurer operating under the parent group Noordlicht Holding U.A.. Its legal insurer identity is Noordlicht Zorgverzekeraar N.V.. Its fictional head office is in Zwolle.

## Corporate profile
- Parent: Noordlicht Holding U.A.
- Legal insurer: Noordlicht Zorgverzekeraar N.V.
- Main brand: Noordlicht Zorg
- Market: Netherlands
- Fictional insured population: Fictional 2027 market share: 6.4%; 510,000 insured.
- Business model: National mutual-style group with a mixed contracted-care network and strong emphasis on coordinated access.

## Insurance product
- Basic policy: Noordlicht Basis (naturapolis)
- Monthly premium: €159.75 per month
- Non-contracted care: 70% of the insurer's average contracted tariff, subject to policy caps
- Supplementary physiotherapy: 9 sessions included in supplementary package Extra; basic-insurance physiotherapy only where statutory eligibility applies.
- Supplementary dental: Extra package reimburses 75% up to €350/year; routine dental care for adults not included in basic policy.

## Care access
- Mental healthcare: Broad national mental-health network; standard referral and triage; published target first contact within 14 days, treatment wait varies by provider.
- Gender-affirming care: Gender-affirming care covered where included in the statutory basic package and clinically indicated; contracted specialist pathway in three university hospital regions; prior coordination required for some treatments.
- Women's healthcare: Maternity care, midwifery and medically necessary gynaecological care through contracted providers; pelvic-floor physiotherapy subject to policy/clinical criteria.
- Disability and rehabilitation: Rehabilitation and medical aids handled through contracted suppliers; case coordinator available after referral for complex care.

## Customer service
Telephone weekdays and secure messaging; average answer time 4m 20s in company report; complaints escalation to independent internal review.

## Criticism and limitations
- Non-contracted reimbursement is limited; gender-care pathway concentrated in three regions; supplementary dental cap is modest.
- Company-reported figures in this entry have not been independently audited.
- Coverage descriptions are summaries, not substitutes for full policy conditions.
