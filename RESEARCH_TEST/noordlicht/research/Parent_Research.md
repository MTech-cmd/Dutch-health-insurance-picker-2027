# Noordlicht Holding U.A. — Parent Company Research

> **Entirely fictional research dossier for practice.** No real-world claim is made.

## 1. Identity
Noordlicht Holding U.A. is the fictional parent group behind Noordlicht Zorg. The operating health insurer is Noordlicht Zorgverzekeraar N.V.. [REF01]

Head office: Zwolle. [REF01]

## 2. Ownership and group structure
The group owns the operating insurer and centrally provides finance, compliance, procurement and digital infrastructure. [REF01]
The group reports no publicly traded shares; it describes itself as a member-owned cooperative. [REF01]

## 3. Size and market position
Fictional insured population and market share: Fictional 2027 market share: 6.4%; 510,000 insured. [REF01]
Fictional annual premium income: €1,917.00 million-equivalent group premium income is not stated; gross written premium is €980 million. [REF01]
These figures are invented and not independently audited.

## 4. Business activities
The group focuses on health insurance and related healthcare administration. Its stated strategy is: National mutual-style group with a mixed contracted-care network and strong emphasis on coordinated access. [REF01]

## 5. Values and corporate responsibility
The company says its priorities are affordability, access and responsible purchasing. [REF05]
No independent verification of its investment, labour, climate or lobbying practices is included in this starter dossier. [REF01]

## 6. Governance and transparency
The annual report describes a supervisory board, executive board and member/customer council. [REF01]
Board composition, remuneration and member-council influence require closer review.

## 7. Reputation and controversies
The fictional customer report records 3.8 formal complaints per 1,000 insured; company-reported, not independently audited. formal complaints per 1,000 insured. [REF04]
This is a company-reported measure; the definition, denominator and complaint severity are not independently validated.

## 8. Initial strengths and weaknesses
**Potential strengths**
- Balanced national reach; relatively clear policy documents; coordinated pathways for complex and gender-affirming care.

**Potential concerns**
- Non-contracted reimbursement is limited; gender-care pathway concentrated in three regions; supplementary dental cap is modest.

## 9. Unknowns / later research

- Actual governance effectiveness.
