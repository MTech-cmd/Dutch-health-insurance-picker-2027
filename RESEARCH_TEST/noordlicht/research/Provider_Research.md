# Noordlicht Zorg — Insurance Provider Research

> **Entirely fictional research dossier for practice.** No real-world claim is made.

## 1. Identity
Public brand: Noordlicht Zorg. [REF01]
Legal insurer: Noordlicht Zorgverzekeraar N.V.. [REF01]
Parent: Noordlicht Holding U.A.. [REF01]

## 2. Basic policy and price
- Policy: Noordlicht Basis (naturapolis). [REF02]
- Monthly premium: €159.75 per month. [REF02]
- Annual premium: €1,917.00. [REF02]
- Assumed statutory deductible for this simulation: €385 statutory deductible assumed in this fictional exercise; voluntary deductible options up to €885. [REF02]
- Non-contracted care: 70% of the insurer's average contracted tariff, subject to policy caps. [REF02]

The reimbursement percentage is not a promise to pay that percentage of any invoice. The policy uses a defined tariff basis and may impose limits and exclusions.

## 3. Provider choice and regional access
National network; strongest in central/eastern Netherlands; 92% of hospitals contracted in the fictional 2027 directory. [REF03]
The directory is fictional and company-reported. A real-world user would need to check the exact provider, treatment location, care type and policy year.

## 4. Mental healthcare
Broad national mental-health network; standard referral and triage; published target first contact within 14 days, treatment wait varies by provider. [REF03]
**Assessment:** The description suggests moderate navigation support, but does not prove short clinical waiting times. [REF03]

## 5. Gender-affirming healthcare
Gender-affirming care covered where included in the statutory basic package and clinically indicated; contracted specialist pathway in three university hospital regions; prior coordination required for some treatments. [REF03]
**Questions still to verify:** named providers, contracted legal entities, referral requirements, prior authorisation, reimbursement for non-contracted care, travel costs, waiting times and continuity of care.

## 6. Women's healthcare
Maternity care, midwifery and medically necessary gynaecological care through contracted providers; pelvic-floor physiotherapy subject to policy/clinical criteria. [REF03]
Areas to investigate include gynaecology, endometriosis pathways, menopause care, fertility-related exclusions, pregnancy and birth care, pelvic-floor physiotherapy and provider availability.

## 7. Disability, chronic illness and rehabilitation
Rehabilitation and medical aids handled through contracted suppliers; case coordinator available after referral for complex care. [REF03]
Investigate aids, rehabilitation, home care interfaces, case management, accessibility of digital services and support for complex or recurring care.

## 8. Physiotherapy
9 sessions included in supplementary package Extra; basic-insurance physiotherapy only where statutory eligibility applies. [REF02]
Basic-policy eligibility and supplementary-policy sessions must be kept separate. Check annual limits, provider contracts and whether unused sessions carry over.

## 9. Dental care
Extra package reimburses 75% up to €350/year; routine dental care for adults not included in basic policy. [REF02]
Check waiting periods, annual caps, reimbursement percentages, exclusions, medical selection and whether major treatment plans are covered.

## 10. Customer service and complaints
Telephone weekdays and secure messaging; average answer time 4m 20s in company report; complaints escalation to independent internal review. [REF04]
Reported complaint rate: 3.8 formal complaints per 1,000 insured; company-reported, not independently audited. [REF04]
The figure is company-reported and should not be compared with other companies unless definitions and measurement methods match.

## 11. Strengths and weaknesses
**Potential strengths**
- Balanced national reach; relatively clear policy documents; coordinated pathways for complex and gender-affirming care.

**Potential weaknesses**
- Non-contracted reimbursement is limited; gender-care pathway concentrated in three regions; supplementary dental cap is modest.

## 12. Unknowns and verification priorities
- Confirm current provider contracts for the user's actual location.
- Obtain full policy conditions and reimbursement schedules.
- Verify specific care pathways with providers, not only the insurer.
- Check waiting times and referral routes.
- Compare total expected annual costs, not premium alone.
- Check exclusions, authorisation, maximum tariffs and supplementary-policy eligibility.

## Research conclusion
Noordlicht Zorg appears most suitable for a reader who prioritises a balance of national access and coordinated complex-care pathways. This is a provisional interpretation of fictional evidence, not a recommendation. Personal location, current providers and exact treatment needs may change the result.
