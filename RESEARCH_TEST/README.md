# Fictional Dutch Health Insurance Research Lab — 2027

**Everything in this folder is fictional.** The three insurers, parent groups, policy names, prices, legal entities, provider networks, statistics, documents, websites and research findings are invented. They are designed for practising research and comparison, not for real insurance decisions.

## Companies
- Noordlicht Zorg — balanced national network and coordinated complex-care pathways.
- Rivierzorg Direct — lowest premium, digital-first, narrower network.
- Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg — higher premium, strong regional care navigation and broader reimbursement.

## Suggested order
1. Read `COMPARISON.md`.
2. Read each company's `research/Parent_Research.md` and `research/Provider_Research.md`.
3. Browse `website/index.html` and `wiki/Wiki.md`.
4. Inspect PDFs in `Sources/`.
5. Check claims against source IDs. The research articles deliberately distinguish company claims, independent evidence, unknowns and limitations.

## Important limitations
The fictional legal and policy details are not legal advice and do not model Dutch law perfectly. In particular, the assumed 2027 deductible is a scenario parameter. Actual statutory coverage, eligibility, contracts, reimbursement and premiums must always be checked against real current sources.
