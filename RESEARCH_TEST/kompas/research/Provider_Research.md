# Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg — Insurance Provider Research

> **Entirely fictional research dossier for practice.** No real-world claim is made.

## 1. Identity
Public brand: Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg. [REF01]
Legal insurer: Kompas Zorgverzekeraar U.A.. [REF01]
Parent: Kompas Gezondheid Groep U.A.. [REF01]

## 2. Basic policy and price
- Policy: Kompas Ruime Keuze (ruime naturapolis). [REF02]
- Monthly premium: €168.90 per month. [REF02]
- Annual premium: €2,026.80. [REF02]
- Assumed statutory deductible for this simulation: €385 statutory deductible assumed in this fictional exercise; voluntary deductible options up to €885. [REF02]
- Non-contracted care: 85% of the insurer's average contracted tariff, subject to policy terms and statutory limits. [REF02]

The reimbursement percentage is not a promise to pay that percentage of any invoice. The policy uses a defined tariff basis and may impose limits and exclusions.

## 3. Provider choice and regional access
Broad in Utrecht, Gelderland and Flevoland; more limited in remote northern and southern areas; 95% of hospitals contracted in fictional 2027 directory. [REF03]
The directory is fictional and company-reported. A real-world user would need to check the exact provider, treatment location, care type and policy year.

## 4. Mental healthcare
Dedicated care-navigation team; broad regional mental-health contracting; offers wait-list mediation, but treatment availability still depends on providers. [REF03]
**Assessment:** The description suggests stronger navigation support, but does not prove short clinical waiting times. [REF03]

## 5. Gender-affirming healthcare
Contracted referral routes for gender-affirming assessment and treatment through partner centres; named care navigator; prior approval required for specified procedures. [REF03]
**Questions still to verify:** named providers, contracted legal entities, referral requirements, prior authorisation, reimbursement for non-contracted care, travel costs, waiting times and continuity of care.

## 6. Women's healthcare
Broad midwifery and hospital network in core provinces; pelvic-health pathway includes contracted pelvic-floor physiotherapists; fewer providers outside core region. [REF03]
Areas to investigate include gynaecology, endometriosis pathways, menopause care, fertility-related exclusions, pregnancy and birth care, pelvic-floor physiotherapy and provider availability.

## 7. Disability, chronic illness and rehabilitation
Strong rehabilitation coordination and assistive-device support; named case manager for eligible complex-care cases. [REF03]
Investigate aids, rehabilitation, home care interfaces, case management, accessibility of digital services and support for complex or recurring care.

## 8. Physiotherapy
12 sessions in supplementary package Plus; additional coverage for specified chronic conditions subject to terms. [REF02]
Basic-policy eligibility and supplementary-policy sessions must be kept separate. Check annual limits, provider contracts and whether unused sessions carry over.

## 9. Dental care
Dental add-on reimburses 80% up to €500/year; higher premium and exclusions for some pre-existing treatment plans. [REF02]
Check waiting periods, annual caps, reimbursement percentages, exclusions, medical selection and whether major treatment plans are covered.

## 10. Customer service and complaints
Telephone, local service desks in core provinces and digital channels; company reports 3m 10s average telephone wait. [REF04]
Reported complaint rate: 3.1 formal complaints per 1,000 insured; company-reported, not independently audited. [REF04]
The figure is company-reported and should not be compared with other companies unless definitions and measurement methods match.

## 11. Strengths and weaknesses
**Potential strengths**
- Strong complex-care navigation; higher non-contracted reimbursement; useful physio and dental options.

**Potential weaknesses**
- Highest premium; regional strength is uneven; some supplementary dental exclusions; care navigation does not guarantee shorter clinical waits.

## 12. Unknowns and verification priorities
- Confirm current provider contracts for the user's actual location.
- Obtain full policy conditions and reimbursement schedules.
- Verify specific care pathways with providers, not only the insurer.
- Check waiting times and referral routes.
- Compare total expected annual costs, not premium alone.
- Check exclusions, authorisation, maximum tariffs and supplementary-policy eligibility.

## Research conclusion
Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg appears most suitable for a reader who prioritises care navigation and broader reimbursement in its core regions. This is a provisional interpretation of fictional evidence, not a recommendation. Personal location, current providers and exact treatment needs may change the result.
