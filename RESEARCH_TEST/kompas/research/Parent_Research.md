# Kompas Gezondheid Groep U.A. — Parent Company Research

> **Entirely fictional research dossier for practice.** No real-world claim is made.

## 1. Identity
Kompas Gezondheid Groep U.A. is the fictional parent group behind Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg. The operating health insurer is Kompas Zorgverzekeraar U.A.. [REF01]

Head office: Amersfoort. [REF01]

## 2. Ownership and group structure
The group owns the operating insurer and centrally provides finance, compliance, procurement and digital infrastructure. [REF01]
The group reports no publicly traded shares; it describes itself as a member-owned cooperative. [REF01]

## 3. Size and market position
Fictional insured population and market share: Fictional 2027 market share: 3.2%; 250,000 insured. [REF01]
Fictional annual premium income: €2,026.80 million-equivalent group premium income is not stated; gross written premium is €510 million. [REF01]
These figures are invented and not independently audited.

## 4. Business activities
The group focuses on health insurance and related healthcare administration. Its stated strategy is: Cooperative insurer with regional roots, broader provider contracting in its core provinces and active care-navigation services. [REF01]

## 5. Values and corporate responsibility
The company says its priorities are affordability, access and responsible purchasing. [REF05]
No independent verification of its investment, labour, climate or lobbying practices is included in this starter dossier. [REF01]

## 6. Governance and transparency
The annual report describes a supervisory board, executive board and member/customer council. [REF01]
Board composition, remuneration and member-council influence require closer review.

## 7. Reputation and controversies
The fictional customer report records 3.1 formal complaints per 1,000 insured; company-reported, not independently audited. formal complaints per 1,000 insured. [REF04]
This is a company-reported measure; the definition, denominator and complaint severity are not independently validated.

## 8. Initial strengths and weaknesses
**Potential strengths**
- Strong complex-care navigation; higher non-contracted reimbursement; useful physio and dental options.

**Potential concerns**
- Highest premium; regional strength is uneven; some supplementary dental exclusions; care navigation does not guarantee shorter clinical waits.

## 9. Unknowns / later research

- Climate transition plan and financed emissions.
- Labour practices and collective bargaining.
- Political lobbying and donations.
- Independent complaints and satisfaction data.
- Actual governance effectiveness.
