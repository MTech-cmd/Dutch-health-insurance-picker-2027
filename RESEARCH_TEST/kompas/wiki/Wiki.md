# Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg — Fictional Wiki

> **Fictional training entry.** This is not a real company or a real Wikipedia article.

## Overview
Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg is a fictional Dutch health insurer operating under the parent group Kompas Gezondheid Groep U.A.. Its legal insurer identity is Kompas Zorgverzekeraar U.A.. Its fictional head office is in Amersfoort.

## Corporate profile
- Parent: Kompas Gezondheid Groep U.A.
- Legal insurer: Kompas Zorgverzekeraar U.A.
- Main brand: Kompas Coöperatieve Zorg
- Market: Netherlands
- Fictional insured population: Fictional 2027 market share: 3.2%; 250,000 insured.
- Business model: Cooperative insurer with regional roots, broader provider contracting in its core provinces and active care-navigation services.

## Insurance product
- Basic policy: Kompas Ruime Keuze (ruime naturapolis)
- Monthly premium: €168.90 per month
- Non-contracted care: 85% of the insurer's average contracted tariff, subject to policy terms and statutory limits
- Supplementary physiotherapy: 12 sessions in supplementary package Plus; additional coverage for specified chronic conditions subject to terms.
- Supplementary dental: Dental add-on reimburses 80% up to €500/year; higher premium and exclusions for some pre-existing treatment plans.

## Care access
- Mental healthcare: Dedicated care-navigation team; broad regional mental-health contracting; offers wait-list mediation, but treatment availability still depends on providers.
- Gender-affirming care: Contracted referral routes for gender-affirming assessment and treatment through partner centres; named care navigator; prior approval required for specified procedures.
- Women's healthcare: Broad midwifery and hospital network in core provinces; pelvic-health pathway includes contracted pelvic-floor physiotherapists; fewer providers outside core region.
- Disability and rehabilitation: Strong rehabilitation coordination and assistive-device support; named case manager for eligible complex-care cases.

## Customer service
Telephone, local service desks in core provinces and digital channels; company reports 3m 10s average telephone wait.

## Criticism and limitations
- Highest premium; regional strength is uneven; some supplementary dental exclusions; care navigation does not guarantee shorter clinical waits.
- Company-reported figures in this entry have not been independently audited.
- Coverage descriptions are summaries, not substitutes for full policy conditions.
