# Research method — starter edition

1. Record the claim in plain language.
2. Attach a source ID and page/section.
3. Identify the publisher and whether it is official, independent, journalistic or user-reported.
4. Separate company claims from independently verified facts.
5. Mark the result Supported, Partly supported, Disputed, Unknown or Outdated.
6. Keep confidence separate: High, Medium, Low or Unknown.
7. For healthcare, always distinguish legal entitlement, contractual entitlement and practical accessibility.
8. Do not infer that a treatment is accessible merely because a website says it is covered.
9. For each specific care need, verify the actual provider, location, contract status, referral, prior approval, reimbursement basis and waiting time.
10. Do not calculate a winner from incomplete or incomparable information.

## Scope of this simulation
The three companies, all source documents and all figures are invented. The PDFs are primary-source simulations created to let a researcher practise cross-checking a company's annual report, policy guide, care access report, service report and website claims.
