# Rivierzorg Direct — Insurance Provider Research

> **Entirely fictional research dossier for practice.** No real-world claim is made.

## 1. Identity
Public brand: Rivierzorg Direct. [REF01]
Legal insurer: Rivierzorg Verzekeringen N.V.. [REF01]
Parent: Riviergroep N.V.. [REF01]

## 2. Basic policy and price
- Policy: Rivier Basis Select (budget naturapolis). [REF02]
- Monthly premium: €145.50 per month. [REF02]
- Annual premium: €1,746.00. [REF02]
- Assumed statutory deductible for this simulation: €385 statutory deductible assumed in this fictional exercise; voluntary deductible options up to €885. [REF02]
- Non-contracted care: 60% of the insurer's average contracted tariff, with additional maximum reimbursement limits. [REF02]

The reimbursement percentage is not a promise to pay that percentage of any invoice. The policy uses a defined tariff basis and may impose limits and exclusions.

## 3. Provider choice and regional access
Narrow preferred network; 78% of hospitals contracted in fictional 2027 directory; selected clinics and suppliers only. [REF03]
The directory is fictional and company-reported. A real-world user would need to check the exact provider, treatment location, care type and policy year.

## 4. Mental healthcare
Digital intake and online therapy options; contracted face-to-face mental-health network is smaller, with longer waits reported in rural areas. [REF03]
**Assessment:** The description suggests more limited navigation support, but does not prove short clinical waiting times. [REF03]

## 5. Gender-affirming healthcare
Covered according to statutory basic-package rules and clinical indication; no dedicated in-house pathway; members are referred to external contracted providers. Non-contracted specialist care can result in substantial out-of-pocket costs. [REF03]
**Questions still to verify:** named providers, contracted legal entities, referral requirements, prior authorisation, reimbursement for non-contracted care, travel costs, waiting times and continuity of care.

## 6. Women's healthcare
Standard maternity and medically necessary gynaecological care through contracted network; fewer independent clinics contracted in northern and rural regions. [REF03]
Areas to investigate include gynaecology, endometriosis pathways, menopause care, fertility-related exclusions, pregnancy and birth care, pelvic-floor physiotherapy and provider availability.

## 7. Disability, chronic illness and rehabilitation
Medical aids available through selected suppliers; online claims process; limited named case-management support. [REF03]
Investigate aids, rehabilitation, home care interfaces, case management, accessibility of digital services and support for complex or recurring care.

## 8. Physiotherapy
6 sessions in supplementary package Start; basic coverage only where statutory eligibility applies. [REF02]
Basic-policy eligibility and supplementary-policy sessions must be kept separate. Check annual limits, provider contracts and whether unused sessions carry over.

## 9. Dental care
Dental add-on reimburses 75% up to €250/year; waiting period applies to some major dental treatments. [REF02]
Check waiting periods, annual caps, reimbursement percentages, exclusions, medical selection and whether major treatment plans are covered.

## 10. Customer service and complaints
App and chat available 24/7 for routine questions; telephone support weekdays; company reports 82% of routine claims processed within 2 working days. [REF04]
Reported complaint rate: 5.6 formal complaints per 1,000 insured; company-reported, not independently audited. [REF04]
The figure is company-reported and should not be compared with other companies unless definitions and measurement methods match.

## 11. Strengths and weaknesses
**Potential strengths**
- Lowest premium in this fictional comparison; strong digital tools; quick routine claims.

**Potential weaknesses**
- Narrower network; lower non-contracted reimbursement; fewer in-person support options; less developed complex-care navigation.

## 12. Unknowns and verification priorities
- Confirm current provider contracts for the user's actual location.
- Obtain full policy conditions and reimbursement schedules.
- Verify specific care pathways with providers, not only the insurer.
- Check waiting times and referral routes.
- Compare total expected annual costs, not premium alone.
- Check exclusions, authorisation, maximum tariffs and supplementary-policy eligibility.

## Research conclusion
Rivierzorg Direct appears most suitable for a reader who prioritises low premiums and digital convenience, while accepting a narrower network. This is a provisional interpretation of fictional evidence, not a recommendation. Personal location, current providers and exact treatment needs may change the result.
