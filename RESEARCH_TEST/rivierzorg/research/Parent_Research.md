# Riviergroep N.V. — Parent Company Research

> **Entirely fictional research dossier for practice.** No real-world claim is made.

## 1. Identity
Riviergroep N.V. is the fictional parent group behind Rivierzorg Direct. The operating health insurer is Rivierzorg Verzekeringen N.V.. [REF01]

Head office: Utrecht. [REF01]

## 2. Ownership and group structure
The group owns the operating insurer and centrally provides finance, compliance, procurement and digital infrastructure. [REF01]
The group reports no publicly traded shares; it describes itself as a privately held company. [REF01]

## 3. Size and market position
Fictional insured population and market share: Fictional 2027 market share: 4.1%; 325,000 insured. [REF01]
Fictional annual premium income: €1,746.00 million-equivalent group premium income is not stated; gross written premium is €620 million. [REF01]
These figures are invented and not independently audited.

## 4. Business activities
The group focuses on health insurance and related healthcare administration. Its stated strategy is: Digital-first national insurer focused on low premiums, automation and a narrower preferred-provider network. [REF01]

## 5. Values and corporate responsibility
The company says its priorities are affordability, access and responsible purchasing. [REF05]
No independent verification of its investment, labour, climate or lobbying practices is included in this starter dossier. [REF01]

## 6. Governance and transparency
The annual report describes a supervisory board, executive board and member/customer council. [REF01]
Board composition, remuneration and member-council influence require closer review.

## 7. Reputation and controversies
The fictional customer report records 5.6 formal complaints per 1,000 insured; company-reported, not independently audited. formal complaints per 1,000 insured. [REF04]

## 8. Initial strengths and weaknesses
**Potential strengths**
- Lowest premium in this fictional comparison; strong digital tools; quick routine claims.

**Potential concerns**
- Narrower network; lower non-contracted reimbursement; fewer in-person support options; less developed complex-care navigation.
