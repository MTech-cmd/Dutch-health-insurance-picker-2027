# Rivierzorg Direct — Fictional Wiki

> **Fictional training entry.** This is not a real company or a real Wikipedia article.

## Overview
Rivierzorg Direct is a fictional Dutch health insurer operating under the parent group Riviergroep N.V.. Its legal insurer identity is Rivierzorg Verzekeringen N.V.. Its fictional head office is in Utrecht.

## Corporate profile
- Parent: Riviergroep N.V.
- Legal insurer: Rivierzorg Verzekeringen N.V.
- Main brand: Rivierzorg Direct
- Market: Netherlands
- Fictional insured population: Fictional 2027 market share: 4.1%; 325,000 insured.
- Business model: Digital-first national insurer focused on low premiums, automation and a narrower preferred-provider network.

## Insurance product
- Basic policy: Rivier Basis Select (budget naturapolis)
- Monthly premium: €145.50 per month
- Non-contracted care: 60% of the insurer's average contracted tariff, with additional maximum reimbursement limits
- Supplementary physiotherapy: 6 sessions in supplementary package Start; basic coverage only where statutory eligibility applies.
- Supplementary dental: Dental add-on reimburses 75% up to €250/year; waiting period applies to some major dental treatments.

## Care access
- Mental healthcare: Digital intake and online therapy options; contracted face-to-face mental-health network is smaller, with longer waits reported in rural areas.
- Gender-affirming care: Covered according to statutory basic-package rules and clinical indication; no dedicated in-house pathway; members are referred to external contracted providers. Non-contracted specialist care can result in substantial out-of-pocket costs.
- Women's healthcare: Standard maternity and medically necessary gynaecological care through contracted network; fewer independent clinics contracted in northern and rural regions.
- Disability and rehabilitation: Medical aids available through selected suppliers; online claims process; limited named case-management support.

## Customer service
App and chat available 24/7 for routine questions; telephone support weekdays; company reports 82% of routine claims processed within 2 working days.

## Criticism and limitations
- Narrower network; lower non-contracted reimbursement; fewer in-person support options; less developed complex-care navigation.
- Company-reported figures in this entry have not been independently audited.
- Coverage descriptions are summaries, not substitutes for full policy conditions.
