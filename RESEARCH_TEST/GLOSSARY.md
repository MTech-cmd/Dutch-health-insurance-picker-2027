# Fictional research glossary

- **Parent group:** The wider corporate group that owns or controls an insurer.
- **Provider / insurer:** The entity or brand offering the health insurance product.
- **Legal insurer:** The legal entity named in the policy contract or regulatory register.
- **Risk carrier:** The legal entity that bears the insurance risk for a policy.
- **Brand:** The customer-facing name; it may not be a separate legal entity.
- **Contracted provider:** A provider with an agreement for the relevant care and policy year.
- **Non-contracted care:** Care from a provider without the relevant contract; reimbursement depends on policy conditions.
- **Company-reported:** A statement published by the organisation itself.
- **Independent evidence:** Evidence produced by a source not controlled by the organisation being assessed.
- **Unknown:** Not established by the sources currently collected.
- **Legal entitlement:** What the law provides.
- **Contractual entitlement:** What the policy terms promise.
- **Practical accessibility:** Whether the person can actually obtain care in a reasonable location and timeframe.
